Introduction

About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Exercise and Sport Science at the University of Wisconsin–La Crosse, where I joined the faculty in August 2023. My work sits at the intersection of physical education teacher education (PETE) and online learning, with a particular focus on how the Community of Inquiry (CoI) framework can improve the quality of digital instruction in kinesiology.

Before entering academia, I spent nearly a decade as a Physical Education teacher in Seoul, South Korea, under the Seoul Metropolitan Office of Education. That classroom experience informs how I think about pedagogy, teacher preparation, and the practical challenges of PE instruction at all levels.

I hold a Ph.D. in Kinesiology & Health from Georgia State University (2021), an M.S. in Physical Education from Seoul National University, and a B.S. in Physical Education from Seoul National University.

Scholarly Focus

Research Interests

Physical Education Teacher Education

Preparing effective PE teachers and examining what makes preservice teacher learning successful.

Online & Technology-Enhanced Learning

Designing and evaluating online kinesiology courses, including digital assessment strategies.

Community of Inquiry Framework

Applying the CoI framework to analyze and improve teaching, social, and cognitive presence in online courses.

Scholarship of Teaching & Learning

Investigating evidence-based practices in higher education kinesiology instruction.

Student Attitudes Toward Physical Activity

Exploring how attitudes toward PA and PE shape learning outcomes and participation.

International & Political Landscape of PE

Comparative analysis of physical education policy and trends across national contexts.
